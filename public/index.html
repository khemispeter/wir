About us

Our background

Women Rise Initiatives is a non-profit organization founded by community members who saw how many women and girls were held back by poor health, limited schooling, low income and unsafe surroundings. We started small, with local groups, and have grown through trust and results.

Today we work side by side with communities, local leaders and partners so that change is led by the people who live it.

Our vision

A society where every woman and girl is healthy, educated, safe and free to lead.

Our mission

To empower women, girls and youth through health, education, livelihoods, protection and peace-building programs that last.
